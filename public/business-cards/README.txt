Card artwork for the "payments, handled end to end" section on /business.

Drop files named 01-*, 02-*, 03-* (.jpg/.png/.webp/.avif). They are matched to
the three cards in order by their leading number and picked up at build time by
src/pages/business.astro — no code change needed.

  01-*   Take payments any way they come
  02-*   Build it into your product
  03-*   Know where your money is

Guidance:
  - PORTRAIT crops read best here — roughly 3:4. The cards are tall.
  - ~900px wide is plenty; they render around 380px.
  - Keep the upper third calm: the card title sits over it.
  - Real African businesses, counters, desks, hands on devices.

Any slot left empty falls back to designed brand art, so the row always reads
as a complete composition.
