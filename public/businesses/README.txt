Drop business photos here (.jpg / .png / .webp / .avif).

They are picked up automatically at build time by
src/components/BusinessMarquee.astro and distributed across the three
marquee rows. Any remaining slots are filled with designed category tiles.

Guidance:
  - Landscape crops read best (roughly 4:3 or 3:2).
  - ~600px wide is plenty; cards render at ~190px.
  - Real African merchants, storefronts, markets, and counters.
  - Filenames become the alt text, so name them descriptively:
      accra-fabric-stall.jpg  ->  "Accra fabric stall"
